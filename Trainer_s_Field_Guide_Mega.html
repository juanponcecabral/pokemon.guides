Origins

Where Pokémon came from.

The story of the franchise, then every generation: its region, its games and its Pokémon. Every Pokédex entry also has its own Origin note.

The idea

A boy who collected insects

Satoshi Tajiri grew up in the outskirts of Tokyo, where he spent his childhood catching and collecting insects. As that countryside disappeared, he wanted to give other children the same joy of collecting.

The cable

Trading over a link cable

Tajiri has described the image of an insect crawling along the Game Boy's link cable, which led to the idea of trading creatures between players. It is why there are exclusive Pokémon in each version and why trading matters.

The team

Game Freak and Ken Sugimori

Tajiri founded the studio Game Freak. Ken Sugimori drew the original Pokémon designs, and the first games took about six years to make.

1996

Pocket Monsters

The original name was 'Pocket Monsters', shortened to Pokémon. Red and Green launched in Japan in February 1996, and Red and Blue followed worldwide in 1998.

The secret

Mew, the 151st

Mew was added in secret by a programmer near the end of development, after the 150 Pokémon were already final. It reached players through special events.

Battle gimmicks

Four ways to power up mid-battle.

Different generations of the games added different one-time mechanics. In each of them a trainer can use the gimmick once per battle, so picking the right moment matters as much as the power itself.

Mega Evolution

Generation 6Pokémon X and Y, 2013

A Pokémon holding its matching Mega Stone evolves temporarily when the trainer carries a Key Stone, such as a Mega Bracelet or Mega Ring. The new form lasts for the rest of the battle.

  • Only one Pokémon per trainer can Mega Evolve in a battle.
  • Base stats rise by 100 points in total, and the type or ability can change.
  • Some species have more than one Mega form, such as Charizard's X and Y.
  • In Legends: Z-A it happens in real time once a Mega meter fills. See every form, with its origin and story, in the Mega Hall.
Origin

Introduced in Pokémon X and Y (2013) as the headline mechanic of Generation 6. It gave beloved, fully evolved Pokémon a temporary new form, and it was the first of the series's one-per-battle gimmicks. It was expanded in the Hoenn remakes, left out of Sword and Shield, and returned in Legends: Z-A (2025).

Story. In Kalos, Mega Evolution is a bond between trainer and Pokémon, studied by Professor Sycamore. The Mega Hall below has the full origin, stories and every Mega form.

Z-Moves

Generation 7Pokémon Sun and Moon, 2016

A trainer wearing a Z-Ring can have a Pokémon holding a Z-Crystal turn one compatible move into a Z-Move, a single powerful attack that can be used once per battle.

  • A generic Z-Move needs the Z-Crystal that matches the type of the move being upgraded.
  • Each of the 18 types has its own Z-Move name, listed in the picker.
  • Certain Pokémon have signature Z-Moves, such as Pikachu's Catastropika.
Origin

Introduced in Pokémon Sun and Moon (2016) as the signature mechanic of Generation 7. A trainer wearing a Z-Ring channels the power of a Z-Crystal through one move, sealing it in a dramatic pose called the Z-Move dance.

Story. In Alola, Z-Power is a tradition tied to the island challenge. The Z-Crystals are earned through the island trials and the Totem Pokémon, and the Z-Ring marks a trainer who has the bond to use them.

Dynamax and Gigantamax

Generation 8Pokémon Sword and Shield, 2019

With a Dynamax Band, a Pokémon grows to a giant size for three turns. Its HP rises and its moves turn into Max Moves. Some species can Gigantamax instead and take on a unique form.

  • It lasts three turns, then the Pokémon returns to normal size.
  • The HP multiplier is ×1.5 at Dynamax Level 0 and ×2 at Level 10.
  • Max Moves can also change the weather or terrain, or shift stats.
  • Gigantamax forms exist for select species, including Charizard, Pikachu and Eevee.
Origin

Introduced in Pokémon Sword and Shield (2019) as the signature mechanic of Generation 8. It was built for Galar's Gym Challenge, a sporting culture of giant stadiums where a Dynamaxed Pokémon becomes a spectacle for the crowd. Gigantamax adds unique forms for select species.

Story. Galar's legend says Dynamax energy comes from the meteorite that brought Eternatus about 20,000 years ago. Chairman Rose hoped to harness it to solve the region's energy crisis, which leads to the Darkest Day.

Terastallization

Generation 9Pokémon Scarlet and Violet, 2022

With a Tera Orb, a Pokémon becomes crystallized and changes its defensive type to its Tera Type. Each Pokémon has one Tera Type, which can be any of the 18 types.

  • It can be used once per battle and stays active until the battle ends.
  • Because the defensive type changes, so do the weaknesses and resistances.
  • Moves of the original type and of the Tera Type both keep the same-type bonus.
  • Terapagos has a special Stellar Tera Type.
Origin

Introduced in Pokémon Scarlet and Violet (2022) as the signature mechanic of Generation 9. A Pokémon crystallizes in the shape of a Tera Type, wearing a jeweled crown that changes how it fights. It was added to wild Tera Raid Battles too.

Story. In Paldea, the Terastal phenomenon is tied to the energy of the Great Crater in the middle of the region. Its source is explored in The Teal Mask expansion, where Terapagos shows the origin of the crystals.

The gimmicks timeline

Since Generation 6, each new generation has introduced its own one-time battle mechanic.

  1. 2013 · Generation 6Mega Evolution

    Kalos. A Key Stone and Mega Stone unlock a temporary evolution.

  2. 2016 · Generation 7Z-Moves

    Alola. A Z-Ring and Z-Crystal turn a move into a once-per-battle super attack.

  3. 2019 · Generation 8Dynamax and Gigantamax

    Galar. A Dynamax Band makes a Pokémon giant for three turns.

  4. 2022 · Generation 9Terastallization

    Paldea. A Tera Orb crystallizes a Pokémon and changes its type.

  5. 2025 · Legends: Z-AMega Evolution returns

    The oldest of the four comes back in Lumiose City.

Where Mega Evolution came from, and every Mega form.

The origin of the mechanic, the stories behind it, and a browsable list of every Mega form with its stats, typing and story. Open any card for a full comparison with the base Pokémon.

Origin

What Mega Evolution is

A temporary evolution that happens during battle. It needs a bond between trainer and Pokémon, a Key Stone worn by the trainer (a Mega Bracelet or Mega Ring) and a Mega Stone held by the Pokémon. It ends when the battle does.

Key StoneWorn by the trainer. It reacts to the bond with the Pokémon.
Mega StoneHeld by the Pokémon, and made for one species (or one form).
BondThe games and anime present trust as the real trigger.
TemporaryThe Pokémon returns to normal when the battle is over.
  1. 2013 · Pokémon X and YDebut in Kalos

    Mega Evolution is introduced as the big new mechanic of Generation 6. Professor Sycamore researches it, and 28 Mega forms are available across the games.

  2. 2013 · X and Y post-gameMewtwo gets two Megas

    Mega Mewtwo X and Mega Mewtwo Y turn one Legendary into two very different fighters, one physical and one special. Charizard gets the same X and Y treatment.

  3. 2014 · Omega Ruby and Alpha SapphireTwenty more, and Mega Rayquaza

    The Hoenn remakes bring the total to 48 Mega forms. Mega Rayquaza is special: it needs no Mega Stone, only the move Dragon Ascent.

  4. 2016 · Sun and MoonZ-Moves arrive

    Generation 7 keeps Mega Evolution and adds Z-Moves beside it.

  5. 2019 · Sword and ShieldMega Evolution is set aside

    Generation 8 drops it in favor of Dynamax and Gigantamax, and it is gone from the main games for years.

  6. 2025 · Pokémon Legends: Z-AThe return, in Lumiose City

    Mega Evolution comes back in a real-time battle game set in Lumiose City, with a wave of brand-new Megas for Pokémon that never had one. The Mega Dimension expansion later in 2025 adds still more.

Stories

Kalos legend

AZ and the Eternal Flower

According to the backstory of X and Y, about 3,000 years ago a king named AZ lost his beloved Floette in a war, and built a machine meant to bring her back. That machine became the ultimate weapon. Floette later became the Eternal Flower, and Z-A gives it a Mega form.

X and Y

Professor Sycamore's research

Kalos's famous professor studies Mega Evolution and the bond that triggers it. It is his research that gives the player the Mega Ring, and it is the reason Mega Evolution takes center stage in the region.

X and Y

The Mewtwo question

Mewtwo was engineered from Mew's genes and is already one of the strongest Pokémon. Mega Evolution lets it take two paths: a fist-first fighter in X and a pure-mind caster in Y.

Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire

Rayquaza and the meteor

In the Delta Episode, a meteoroid threatens Hoenn. Zinnia, who comes from a dragon-tamer lineage, calls on Rayquaza, which Mega Evolves to break the meteoroid apart.

On the battlefield

Trainers who Mega Evolve

Powerful opponents use Megas too: Champion Diantha leads with Mega Gardevoir, Steven Stone brings Mega Metagross in the Hoenn remakes, and Team Flare's Lysandre has Mega Gyarados.

Anime

On screen

The anime gives Mega Evolution a story of its own, with trainers like Alain and his Mega Charizard X showing how deeply a trainer and Pokémon have to trust one another.

Legends: Z-A

Lumiose City and the rogue Megas

Set in the middle of Lumiose City's big redevelopment, Z-A has the Z-A Royale tournament and wild "rogue" Pokémon that have Mega Evolved on their own. The Mega Dimension expansion takes the story into Hyperspace Lumiose.

Legends

The origin and stories of the Legendary Pokémon.

Every region has its myths. Here are the groups that tell them, then every Legendary, Mythical and Ultra Beast with its story.

Legendary

Rare, and part of the story

One of a kind in the games, found in a special place, and usually at the center of a region's legend. They are generally the ones that appear on the box.

Mythical

Gifts from events

Pokémon that are rarely available in the main game and mostly arrive through special events, like Mew, the secret 151st.

Ultra Beast

Visitors from other worlds

Introduced in Sun and Moon, they arrive from other dimensions through Ultra Wormholes and have no ordinary place in Alola's ecosystem.
